Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a Tracking Number & Where Can I Find It?
A tracking number is a unique ID assigned to your shipment. You can find it on your shipping confirmation email, receipt, or the shipping label.
When will my tracking information appear?
Tracking information typically appears within 24 hours after your shipment is processed and picked up by DHL.
Why is my tracking number not working?
Ensure you've entered the number correctly. If it still doesn't work, please contact DHL customer support for assistance.
What does "In Transit" mean?
"In Transit" means your shipment is on its way to the destination and is moving through the DHL network.
Will I receive email updates?
Yes. If you provide your email when tracking, you'll receive automatic updates about your shipment status.
